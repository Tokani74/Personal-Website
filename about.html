// who i am

I'm an engineer with a builder's mindset. I don't just design systems on paper — I fabricate, assemble, solder, and iterate until they actually work. My projects span the full stack: writing Python scripts to control hardware over radio, designing and etching custom PCBs, and modeling structural components in CAD.

What makes my work distinctive is breadth without shallowness. I've written LAMMPS simulation code for published nanoscale research, designed a two-board custom electronics system for AR glasses from scratch, and built a hand-gesture-controlled aircraft. Each project required deep dives into unfamiliar domains — and I've learned to be comfortable being the least experienced person in the room.

I'm currently looking for internships and engineering roles where I can apply hardware and software skills together, ideally in robotics, embedded systems, or product development.

Electronics Lead

UT Austin Makerspace

As Electronics Lead, I oversee the electronics area of UT Austin's student Makerspace — helping students prototype PCBs, debug circuits, and learn soldering. I apply those same skills to my own projects, including hand-assembling two custom boards for the AR glasses directly at the Makerspace and 3D-printing propellers for the RC plane.
