Mid-Knight Snack

alt.ctrl.GDC

Our controller is a fridge. A fully functional one! It was never designed to guide a knight on a midnight snack run, or to power a live, zero-input-lag game while you reach in for an ice-cold soda, but that’s exactly what it does here. Instead of thumbsticks, you open and close the fridge doors to control the action. Instead of buttons, you grab real food from the correct plate lit up by LEDs inside the fridge once you collect enough of that food in the game, with laser-cut props and magnet switches translating each motion into gameplay. It’s a hands-on, physical experience that feels a little silly but is a whole lot of fun. And as far as we know, it’s the only real-time controller out there that keeps your chicken nuggets frozen and your drinks cold while you play.

I mainly worked on the electronics, music, and video submission.

Collaborated with Ritz Song, Kemblebee, and Harrison Floyd.
