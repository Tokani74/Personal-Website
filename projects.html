01 Personal Project Ongoing

AR Speech-to-Text Glasses

Glasses that display real-time subtitles on the lens. Onboard mic captures speech → Bluetooth to phone for STT processing → transcribed text displayed back on lens.

KiCadOnshapePCB DesignBluetoothEmbedded Systems

What I Built

  • Custom CAD glasses frame (Onshape) with internal wire channels
  • Two hand-assembled PCBs: power board and processing/display board
  • Full KiCad schematics + PCB layout completed in ~2 months
  • Modified open-source smart glasses firmware to fit custom hardware

Obstacle → Solution

Original monolithic PCB couldn't deliver consistent power. Split into two purpose-built boards for easier troubleshooting — several power issues since resolved. Development ongoing.

03 Personal Project Completed

Hand-Tracking RC Plane

A fully built RC plane controlled by hand gestures — a webcam tracks finger joints via Python/OpenCV, translating movement into servo commands sent by radio to an Arduino onboard the aircraft.

PythonOpenCVArduinoRadio TX/RX3D PrintingFoam Board

What I Built

  • Foam board airframe with hand-cut wings, fuselage, and control surfaces
  • 3D-printed propellers (UT Austin Makerspace)
  • Python/OpenCV hand-tracking: finger position maps to throttle and direction
  • Dual-Arduino radio system — ground TX parses OpenCV output, onboard RX drives servos

Lesson Learned

The plane flew — but the motor/propeller/weight ratio was found through trial and error. Key takeaway: use aerodynamic lift calculations upfront rather than physical iteration. Saves time and material.

04 Team Competition Completed

Battlebot

A combat robot with a steel spinning weapon and hard PETG/TPU chassis — designed in SolidWorks and largely executed solo despite being a team project.

SolidWorks3D PrintingPETG / TPUESCLiPo SystemsRadio TX/RX

What I Built

  • Full SolidWorks CAD: spinning weapon, PETG/TPU chassis, drivetrain, electronics bay
  • 3D-printed chassis with ramped surface, structural trusses, fillets, bearing seats
  • Electronics: LiPo, ESC, radio TX/RX, independent drive motors, weapon motor
  • Iterated on drivetrain — next version uses pulley rather than direct hub drive

Obstacles

  • No prior battlebot experience on the team
  • Team conflicts left me as primary contributor
  • Strict weight and size constraints required many revision cycles

Lesson

Start the prototype much earlier and build in explicit buffer for revisions and unexpected solo work.
